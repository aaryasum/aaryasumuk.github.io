<div class="ed-eyebrow">Robot Learning · Manipulation · Tactile Sensing · Human-Robot Interaction</div>
<h1 class="ed-h1">Hi, I'm Aarya.</h1>

<div class="ed-intro">
  <p>I recently graduated from Stanford University with an M.S. in Robotics. I'm interested in grounded robot intelligence: how robotic policies learn spatial, semantic, and physical structure from perception, language, simulation, and real-world interaction. I'm especially drawn to contact-rich manipulation as a testbed for generalization, failure recovery, and skill learning.</p>
  <p>At Stanford, I most recently worked in the <a href="https://arm.stanford.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ARM Lab</a> with <a href="https://monroekennedy3.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Monroe Kennedy III</a>, exploring how visuotactile world models can give robots a richer sense of contact and force. Before that, I was in the <a href="https://iprl.stanford.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">IPRL Lab</a> with <a href="https://web.stanford.edu/~bohg/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Jeannette Bohg</a>, developing learning-based methods for bimanual, contact-rich assembly. I got my start in the <a href="https://charm.stanford.edu/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">CHARM Lab</a> with <a href="https://profiles.stanford.edu/allison-okamura" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Prof. Allison Okamura</a>, working on haptic interfaces and human-robot interaction.</p>
</div>

<div class="ed-callout">
  <span class="ed-callout__lead">Before Stanford,</span> I studied Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras, focusing on assistive technologies and mechatronic systems. Outside the lab, I sang with <a href="https://www.stanfordcounterpoint.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Stanford Counterpoint</a> (an all-women a cappella group), played guitar, and swam. If any of this resonates, feel free to reach out. I'm always happy to chat!
</div>
