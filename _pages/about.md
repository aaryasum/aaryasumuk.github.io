# Welcome
*Robot Learning · Contact-Rich Manipulation · Vision & Touch*

Hi! I recently graduated from Stanford University with an M.S. in Robotics. I’m interested in building robots that can learn from vision, touch, and interaction with the physical world. My research explores grounded robot intelligence through contact-rich manipulation, visuotactile learning, and world models.

At Stanford, I conducted research in the [IPRL Lab](https://iprl.stanford.edu/) under the supervision of [Prof. Jeannette Bohg](https://web.stanford.edu/~bohg/), where I worked on learning-based methods for contact-rich, bimanual assembly. I later joined the ARM Lab with Prof. Monroe Kennedy III, where I explored visuotactile world models for robotic manipulation. I also worked with [Prof. Allison Okamura](https://profiles.stanford.edu/allison-okamura) in the [CHARM Lab](https://charm.stanford.edu/) on haptic interfaces and human–robot interaction.

Before Stanford, I completed my undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering at IIT Madras, where my research focused on assistive technologies and mechatronic system design. Outside of research, I’m a member of [Stanford Counterpoint](https://www.stanfordcounterpoint.com/), an all-women a cappella group, and I enjoy playing the guitar, swimming, and occasionally attempting to teach robots new tricks.

If any of my work or interests resonate with you, feel free to reach out—I’m always happy to chat!
