In Spring 2026, I was a Course Assistant for **ENGR 15: Dynamics**, taught by Prof. Monroe Kennedy.

ENGR 15 is an undergraduate course on the application of Newton's Laws to 2-D and 3-D static and dynamic problems. Topics include particle and rigid body dynamics, free body diagrams, and equations of motion, with applications to mechanical, biomechanical, and aerospace systems. The course also covers computer numerical solutions and dynamic system response.
