In Winter 2026, I was a Course Assistant for **CS334: Robots and Art**, taught by Catie Cuan and Prof. Oussama Khatib.

CS334 is an interdisciplinary, project-based course exploring creative applications of robotics at the intersection of engineering, art, and design. Students work in teams to conceive and build original robotic systems, ranging from interactive installations to performance-based projects, culminating in a public exhibition at the Stanford Robotics Center.
