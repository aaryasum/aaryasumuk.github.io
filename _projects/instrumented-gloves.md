Designed a sensor-equipped glove with real-time 3D modeling for **sign language recognition**. Combined **flex sensors, IMU, and EMG** with an ESP32 for wireless joint angle tracking in MATLAB. Simulated a real-time 3D hand model and validated accuracy using the **VICON motion capture system**.
