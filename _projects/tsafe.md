Developing **T-SAFE**, a tactile-aware recovery framework for contact-rich nut assembly. The system diagnoses failures including micro-slip, misalignment, grasp voids, obstructions, and surface irregularities. Built a **Robosuite + tactile simulation pipeline** with scripted failure injection and supervised diagnosis baselines. Prototyping diagnosis-conditioned recovery via residual and primitive corrective actions, with a path toward sim-to-real deployment on **SO-101**.
