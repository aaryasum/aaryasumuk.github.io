Engineered a wearable **haptic feedback system** with dual linear actuators for force and motion sensing. Developed real-time mapping from **IMU orientation** to actuator response for proprioceptive experiments. Integrated **motion capture and flex sensors** to benchmark feedback accuracy and latency.
