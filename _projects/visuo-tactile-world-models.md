Training **action-conditioned visuo-tactile world models** for contact-rich tabletop manipulation, extending interactive world simulators with **DenseTact tactile feedback**. Investigating whether tactile-conditioned rollouts improve policy learning, long-horizon prediction, and offline policy evaluation. Collecting multimodal demonstrations using a custom **UMI-style gripper**, **iPhone RGB-D**, **AprilTag-based gripper tracking**, and lab-built **DenseTact sensors**.
