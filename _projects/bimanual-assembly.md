Built a full **teleoperation and data pipeline** for dual Franka arms using Oculus and a custom OSC controller, with self and dual-arm collision avoidance for safe bimanual motion. Trained and deployed **diffusion policy**, **SERL**, and **HIL-SERL** for contact-rich assembly. Investigated NIST board tasks using IL with residual RL, combining force cues and active perception.
